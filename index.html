Frequently Asked Questions

Browse general answers to common vendor questions.

Where can I find NSPCL tender opportunities?

Check the official NSPCL website and the Central Public Procurement Portal. Always read the individual tender notice for its deadlines, eligibility requirements and submission process.

NSPCL Website | CPPP Portal

What is EMD in a tender?

EMD stands for Earnest Money Deposit. Whether it is required, its amount, and any exemptions depend on the specific tender conditions and applicable procurement rules.

Where can I access GeM?

Use the official Government e-Marketplace website for seller registration, catalogue information and procurement-related services.

Visit GeM

How can I update my vendor details?

Follow the applicable portal instructions for updating vendor details. For NSPCL-specific changes, consult the relevant instructions or contact the designated NSPCL department.

How do I submit an invoice?

Follow the purchase order, contract terms and invoice submission instructions applicable to your order. Confirm the required supporting documents and submission channel with the concerned NSPCL department.
