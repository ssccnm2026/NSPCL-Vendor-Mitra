Frequently Asked Questions

Explore common vendor queries or ask Vendor Mitra for guidance.

How can I find NSPCL tender opportunities?

Visit the official NSPCL website and the Central Public Procurement Portal. Review each tender notice for eligibility, deadlines and submission requirements.

NSPCL Website | Central Public Procurement Portal

What should I check before submitting a bid?

Read the complete tender document, verify eligibility and technical requirements, prepare the requested documents, and submit your bid before the deadline specified in the tender.

What is EMD?

EMD stands for Earnest Money Deposit. It may be required as part of a tender, subject to the tender conditions and any applicable exemptions.

Where can I find GeM information?

Visit the Government e-Marketplace portal for official information about seller registration, products, services and procurement.

Visit GeM

How can I update my vendor details?

Follow the applicable vendor registration or procurement portal procedure. For NSPCL-specific changes, refer to the relevant instructions or contact the designated NSPCL department.
