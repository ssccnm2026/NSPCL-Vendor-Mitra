Frequently Asked Questions

Browse general answers to common vendor questions.

Where can I find NSPCL tender opportunities?

Check the official NSPCL website and Central Public Procurement Portal. Read each tender notice for its deadlines, eligibility requirements and submission process.

NSPCL Website | CPPP Portal

What is EMD in a tender?

EMD stands for Earnest Money Deposit. Its requirement, amount and exemptions depend on the specific tender conditions and applicable procurement rules.

Where can I access GeM?

Use the official Government e-Marketplace website for seller registration and procurement services.

Visit GeM

How can I update my vendor details?

Follow the applicable portal instructions. For NSPCL-specific changes, consult the relevant instructions or contact the designated NSPCL department.

How do I submit an invoice?

Follow the purchase order, contract terms and invoice submission instructions applicable to your order. Confirm required supporting documents with the concerned department.
